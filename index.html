How to play & tower guide

Choose a tower, then tap an empty tile off the route. Tap a placed tower to upgrade or sell it. You can build during a wave. Escape cancels selection or closes upgrades.

Basic: affordable rapid fire · Sniper: long range · Cannon: area damage · Frost: slows enemies by 50% for 2 seconds.

Protect the red base through 15 waves. Clearing a wave grants 20 + 2 × wave gold and saves a checkpoint. Load restores the last saved preparation phase. Restart starts a fresh run on the selected map; your checkpoint remains until overwritten.

Meteor: 60 damage to every enemy, once per 30 seconds. Sound is optional. Leaving the tab pauses an active wave; press Resume to continue. Maps can be changed before building or starting a wave.
